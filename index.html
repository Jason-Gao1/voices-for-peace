Who We Are

A Platform for Peace-Building

The Ahmadiyya Muslim Community International, under the guidance of its Caliph Hazrat Mirza Masroor Ahmad, launched the global #VoicesForPeace campaign to bring an end to the war in Gaza and establish lasting peace.


We have held global forums across the world — including at the UK Houses of Parliament, Capitol Hill, and the Scottish Parliament — calling for an immediate and permanent ceasefire, the protection of innocent civilians, and a just resolution.


Join the Movement

What Must Happen

Our Core Demands

🕊️

Permanent Ceasefire

An immediate and lasting ceasefire is a moral imperative. The ongoing conflict has claimed tens of thousands of innocent lives and must be brought to an end now.

🛡️

Protecting the Innocent

Civilian lives must be protected under international law. Every child, woman, and man caught in the crossfire deserves safety, shelter, and access to aid.

⚖️

Justice for All

True peace is only achievable through justice. All parties must be held accountable, and the rights of every people must be upheld equally and without bias.

🌐

Global De-escalation

Major world powers have a responsibility to act. We call on world leaders to prioritise diplomacy and to actively support de-escalation before further catastrophe unfolds.
